SEL0359 - Digital Control

Undergraduate in Electrical Engineering

Workload: 60h

Learning Objectives

This course aims to introduce techniques for the analysis and design of digital control systems. The main topics studied are: discrete-time system representation, discretization of continuous-time systems, stability analysis, controller design, discrete-time state-space representation, state-feedback control design, state observer design, LQR control, and Kalman Filter.

Syllabus

  • 01

    SIGNAL CLASSIFICATION

    Signal classification and quantization characteristics.

  • 02

    SAMPLED SIGNALS

    Mathematical treatment of sampled (discrete) signals.

  • 03

    Z-TRANSFORM

    Z-Transform and its applications in discrete systems.

  • 04

    DISCRETE STABILITY

    Discrete stability criteria and s-z mapping.

  • 05

    STATE-SPACE

    Description of discrete processes in state-space.

  • 06

    PID CONTROLLERS

    Design and implementation of discrete PID controllers.

  • 07

    POLE PLACEMENT

    Generic compensators and pole placement design.

  • 08

    DEAD-BEAT

    Design of dead-beat controllers (finite time).

  • 09

    STATE FEEDBACK

    State feedback control and state observer design.
